Key findings and suggested improvements

Interpretation of the 34 submitted responses

1. Make reporting easy to find

18 respondents (52.9%) do not know where or how to report an issue. Keep the report form easy to access and explain the reporting steps clearly.

2. Provide tracking and feedback

Respondents want a unique issue ID and visible progress. Show stages such as Reported, Assigned, Under Maintenance and Resolved.

3. Support photo evidence

28 respondents (82.4%) support repair photos. Let staff submit evidence and display before/after photos where available.

4. Keep accountability in the workflow

Include station/platform details and officer verification before an issue is finally marked resolved.

Conclusion

The survey indicates a clear need for a more accessible and transparent railway maintenance reporting process. Most respondents viewed RailSafe as useful and supported digital reporting, status tracking and repair evidence. These results support the project’s focus on issue reporting, unique IDs, location details, progress updates and photo-based verification. The survey reflects 34 respondents and should not be treated as representative of every railway passenger.
